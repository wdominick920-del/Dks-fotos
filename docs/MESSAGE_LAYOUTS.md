# Owner-friendly message layouts

> Current behaviour (12 November 2026): Ordinary enquiry handoffs now use concise client/session/package/extra/travel/total details and a reference, without status envelopes, repeated policy paragraphs or consent URLs. Full structured records remain available to expand/copy. Email handoffs use mailto first, with optional Gmail links. Consent and withdrawal retain their exact detailed records.

All messages use plain text so the structure survives Gmail, default mail apps, copy/paste and WhatsApp. Formatting uses short headings and blank lines rather than HTML, wide tables or app-specific markup. Email subjects retain the reference for searching and identify the message type. The website cannot control edits a client makes before sending or the receiving app's fonts.

- General enquiries: reference, client/session details, client message, optional selected estimate, and next step.
- Quote requests: reference, package/coverage, chosen extras, travel breakdown, total/booking fee/balance, policy, and next step.
- Client feedback: reference, client/session, rating, written feedback, and explicit review-publication choice. This remains separate from permission to use photographs.
- Photo-use choices: reference, client/booking, exact choice, all five channel decisions, restrictions/expiry, child authority when relevant, version/date/acknowledgement, and owner verification steps.
- Permission changes: reference, client/booking, requested change, notice/date, and owner action.

When a quote is included in a contact enquiry, duplicate message envelopes, references and optional-consent footers are removed without dropping package, price, extra or travel details. Older quote formats are retained as readable text instead of silently losing their estimates. Consent records avoid a redundant link asking the client to record the choice they just prepared.

Gmail, WhatsApp, default-mail-app fallbacks, enquiry copy and consent copy/download use the same prepared body. Optional configured enquiry/review services also receive formattedMessage alongside individual fields. No real email, WhatsApp message or acknowledgement is sent during testing. Prepared references are not confirmation of receipt or booking, and photo-use choices still require verification. No submission service has been activated by this change.
