05 / Before you book

Booking & payment terms.

Clear expectations from enquiry to delivery. Tap any heading to read more.

Booking fee

30% reserves your date; the balance is due 48 hours before the session unless agreed otherwise.

Confirmation

Your booking is confirmed after the booking fee is received and the written agreement is accepted.

Rescheduling

One date change with at least seven days’ notice, subject to availability and unavoidable expenses.

Cancellations

Refunds and charges are handled under the agreement and applicable South African consumer law; no blanket non-refundable deposit.

Delivery

Standard sessions: approximately 5–7 working days. Weddings: approximately 3–5 weeks. Exact deadlines confirmed in writing.

Editing & images

Professional colour/exposure correction and basic retouching included. RAW files and extensive alterations excluded unless agreed.

Rights & privacy

Personal clients receive personal-use rights; business usage is limited to the agreed licence. Client images are published only with appropriate permission.

Coverage

Event and wedding image quantities are estimates. Overtime, extra locations, permits and special requirements may cost extra.

Prices may change for new enquiries. Your written quotation confirms the final price, image usage rights, travel, deliverables and booking conditions.
