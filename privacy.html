Who we are and how to contact us

DKs Photography is Dominick’s photography business based in Stellenbosch. Contact wdominick920@gmail.com or about your photographs, booking information or privacy choices.

Providing your photography service

Photographs and relevant booking information are used to plan the session, communicate with you, provide the agreed photography and editing, deliver your gallery, and administer the booking. Your quotation and agreement describe the service, delivery and image-use terms.

Promotional use is your choice

Permission to promote DKs Photography is optional and separate from delivering the agreed service. Declining promotion does not affect the agreed service or price. Asking a question or making an enquiry does not require promotional permission.

With appropriate permission, identifiable photographs may appear on the website and portfolio; Facebook and Instagram organic posts; WhatsApp Business status and catalogue; printed marketing; or paid Facebook and Instagram advertisements. These channels require separate choices. Unselected channels are not authorised. This form does not grant permission to tag you or publish your name; that must be agreed separately.

Whose permission can you give?

You can record a choice for yourself or for a child you are legally authorised to represent. A booking organiser cannot automatically consent for all guests. Permission needs to relate to the identifiable person and relevant photographs. If a choice is missing, unclear, anonymous, unverified or cannot be linked to the person and photographs, promotional use is not authorised. “Please ask me first” requires separate approval of specific photographs before publication.

Restrictions and withdrawal

You can restrict channels, exclude particular photographs, specify a permission end date, or change or withdraw your permission by email or WhatsApp. Identify the booking, person, photographs and requested change so the request can be checked and acted on. A withdrawal message contacts DKs Photography; it does not automatically remove already published photographs.

After a verified withdrawal, DKs Photography should stop new promotional use covered by that withdrawal and arrange removal from channels it controls where practicable. This cannot guarantee recovery of screenshots, copies shared by others, third-party caches, or printed material already distributed. Discuss the requested removal and any practical limits directly with DKs Photography.

Storage, access and retention

Booking information, photographs and permission records should be stored appropriately for the service, with access limited to people who need it for agreed work or legitimate administration. Information should be kept only for as long as needed for delivery, booking records, applicable legal requirements and resolving requests or disputes.

Ask DKs Photography for the storage locations, people with access and retention period relevant to your booking. This notice does not promise an unverified security measure, fixed deletion date or guaranteed removal from third-party services. Consent records belong in private booking records, not a public website repository.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable South African law, you may ask about access to your personal information, request correction or deletion where appropriate, object to processing, withdraw optional consent and raise a privacy concern. Contact DKs Photography first for help with a request. You can also consult or complain to South Africa’s Information Regulator. A request may need enough information to verify your connection to the booking; this form does not ask for identity documents or ID numbers.
